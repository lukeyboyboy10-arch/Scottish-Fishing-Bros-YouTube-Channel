Who We Are

Welcome to the official home of the Scottish Fishing Bros! Luke and his co-partner Oscar are young passionate anglers dedicated to exploring the beautiful rivers, lochs, and coastal waters of Scotland.

They are frequently joined by guests such as Luke's father and others. Whether they are chasing wild brown trout in remote hill lochs or sea fishing off the rugged coastlines, they bring you along for every single cast, laugh, and tangle.

Loch & River Sessions

From traditional fly fishing to casting spinner lures across local rivers and wild hill lochs, we target everything that swims in Scotland's iconic freshwater settings.
