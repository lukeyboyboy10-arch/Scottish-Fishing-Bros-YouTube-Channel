Who We Are

Welcome to the official home of the Scottish Fishing Bros! Luke and his co-partner Oscar are young passionate anglers dedicated to exploring the beautiful rivers, lochs and coastal waters of Scotland. They are frequently joined by guests such as lukes father. Whether we are chasing wild brown trout in remote hill lochs, sea fishing off the rugged coastlines, They bring you along for every single cast, laugh, and tangle.
